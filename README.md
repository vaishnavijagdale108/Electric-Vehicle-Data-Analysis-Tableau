# 🚗 Electric Vehicle Data Analysis — Tableau

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Electric Vehicle (EV) Data Analysis Dashboard** built using **Tableau**.

The dashboard analyzes electric vehicle population data to understand EV adoption trends, vehicle distribution across states, leading manufacturers and models, electric vehicle types, and CAFV eligibility.

The project includes key performance indicators (KPIs), interactive filters, geographical analysis, trend analysis, manufacturer analysis, and vehicle-level insights.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The objective of this project is to analyze the electric vehicle landscape and identify important patterns in EV adoption.

The analysis focuses on:

- Overall electric vehicle population
- Average electric range
- Battery Electric Vehicle (BEV) adoption
- Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicle (PHEV) adoption
- EV adoption trends by model year
- Geographical distribution of EVs by state
- Leading EV manufacturers
- CAFV eligibility distribution
- Popular EV models

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Total Vehicles by Model Year
A line/area chart showing the distribution and growth of electric vehicles across model years from 2010 onwards.

### 2. Total Vehicles by State
A geographical map showing the distribution of electric vehicles across different states.

### 3. Top 10 Total Vehicles by Make
A bar chart highlighting the top 10 EV manufacturers based on the number of vehicles.

### 4. Total Vehicles by CAFV Eligibility
A donut chart showing the distribution of vehicles based on Clean Alternative Fuel Vehicle (CAFV) eligibility.

### 5. Total Vehicles by Model
A detailed table showing popular EV models along with their manufacturer, EV type, vehicle count, and percentage contribution.

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard provides interactive filters that allow users to explore the data based on:

- CAFV Eligibility
- EV Type
- Model
- State

These filters allow users to dynamically analyze different segments of the EV population.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) represent the majority of vehicles in the dataset, accounting for approximately **77.6%** of the total EV population.
- Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEVs) account for approximately **22.4%**.
- The dashboard shows a strong increase in EV adoption across newer model years.
- Tesla is the leading manufacturer in the dataset based on total vehicle count.
- EV adoption varies significantly across states.
- The dashboard provides visibility into CAFV eligibility and its distribution across the EV population.
